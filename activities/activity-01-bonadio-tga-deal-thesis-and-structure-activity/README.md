# Activity 1: Bonadio–TGA Deal Thesis and Structure Activity

**TSC mapping:** K1 · A1 · A6  
**Objective:** Apply financial instruments to shape a deal structure (K1, A1, A6)  
**Expected output:** Deal Thesis and Structure Decision Page (PDF or DOCX)  
**Primary source:** https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2026/08/06/credit-risk-management-firm-joins-the-bonadio-group/188098/

## Goal

Use the announced Bonadio–TGA professional-services transaction to build a defensible strategic thesis and term-sheet architecture.

## Real-world scenario

On 6 August 2026, CPA Practice Advisor reported that The Bonadio Group would add T. Gschwender & Associates (TGA), a Syracuse provider of credit-risk management services to financial institutions. The transaction was expected to close on 16 August 2026; financial terms were not disclosed. TGA was to join Bonadio's internal audit business unit within Advisory & Consulting.

You are on Bonadio's deal team before the expected closing. Leadership wants a concise view of why TGA fits, which capabilities create value, and how a structure could protect client continuity and specialist talent when the actual terms are unknown.

## Evidence boundary

Reported deal facts and learner assumptions must never be mixed. Financial terms not disclosed by the parties are treated only as training assumptions.

### Training assumptions / guardrails

- No purchase price, revenue, EBITDA or ownership terms were reported; do not infer them.
- For comparison only, assume the seller values continuity, client service and continued management participation.
- Treat every proposed instrument as a learner recommendation, not an account of the actual transaction.

## Questions

1. Which facts support strategic fit, and which important facts remain unknown?
2. Would cash, rollover equity, an earn-out, a seller note or a holdback best allocate risk? Why?
3. What value could scale create without weakening TGA's specialist edge?
4. What conditions would make the team pause or walk away?

## Detailed activity procedure

### Step 1: Read the source and separate verified facts from interpretation

Create two columns: Reported fact | Deal-team inference. Record the publication and expected-close dates.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 2: Write a one-sentence deal thesis

Complete: Bonadio should pursue TGA because [capability/client/geography], creating value through [specific mechanism], provided [critical risk] is controlled.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 3: Score strategic fit

Rate capability fit, client adjacency, specialist differentiation, cultural fit, execution complexity and integration capacity from 1–5; justify every score.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 4: Map value creation

Identify revenue, cost, capital and resilience levers. Reject any lever that lacks an owner or evidence path.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 5: Design the consideration mix

Compare cash, rollover equity, earn-out, seller note and escrow/holdback against certainty, retention, financing, tax and dispute risk.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 6: Draft risk allocation

Propose at least three protections such as working-capital adjustment, warranties, indemnity, escrow, retention condition or client-revenue earn-out.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 7: Run the challenge meeting

Assign Deal Sponsor, Finance, Legal/Risk and Integration roles. Each role states one objection and one required condition.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 8: Finalise the decision page

State recommendation, preferred structure, top three risks, mitigations and walk-away triggers. Label all assumptions.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

## Deliverable

Deal Thesis and Structure Decision Page (PDF or DOCX)

## Acceptance criteria

The page distinguishes facts from assumptions, proposes at least two instruments, links each term to a risk or value driver, and includes an explicit go/conditional/no-go recommendation.

## Reflection and debrief questions

1. Which assumption changed your recommendation most, and why?
2. What evidence would you request before relying on the result?
3. Which stakeholder owns the next decision or mitigation?
4. How does this activity feed the next stage of the M&A lifecycle?
