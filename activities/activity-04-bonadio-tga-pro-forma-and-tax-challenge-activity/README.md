# Activity 4: Bonadio–TGA Pro Forma and Tax Challenge Activity

**TSC mapping:** K5 · A1 · A2  
**Objective:** Evaluate financial and tax implications and act as checks and balances (K5, A1, A2)  
**Expected output:** Pro Forma Impact Pack and Board Challenge Paper  
**Primary source:** https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2026/08/06/credit-risk-management-firm-joins-the-bonadio-group/188098/

## Goal

Trace an illustrative acquisition through the income statement, balance sheet and cash flow, then challenge tax and control assumptions.

## Real-world scenario

On 6 August 2026, CPA Practice Advisor reported that The Bonadio Group would add T. Gschwender & Associates (TGA), a Syracuse provider of credit-risk management services to financial institutions. The transaction was expected to close on 16 August 2026; financial terms were not disclosed. TGA was to join Bonadio's internal audit business unit within Advisory & Consulting.

Before board approval, the controller, tax lead and independent risk reviewer must explain how the illustrative structure changes profit, cash, leverage and tax—and which issues cannot be resolved from public information.

## Evidence boundary

Reported deal facts and learner assumptions must never be mixed. Financial terms not disclosed by the parties are treated only as training assumptions.

### Training assumptions / guardrails

- Training case only: purchase enterprise value US$16.0m; consideration 60% cash/debt and 40% buyer equity.
- Illustrative identifiable intangible assets: client relationships US$4.0m and trade name US$1.0m; assumed useful lives 8 and 5 years.
- Illustrative fair-value adjustment to other net assets: US$1.5m; deferred-tax rate 24%.
- Use this as an accounting and governance simulation; obtain jurisdiction-specific accounting, legal and tax advice for a real deal.

## Questions

1. What drives goodwill under the training PPA?
2. How do financing, intangible amortisation and integration charges affect earnings and cash differently?
3. Which tax attributes or exposures need documentary evidence?
4. What approval conditions should the independent review team impose?

## Detailed activity procedure

### Step 1: Build sources and uses

Show enterprise value, debt-like/cash adjustments, fees, consideration mix and financing. Confirm sources equal uses.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 2: Sketch purchase-price allocation

Allocate consideration to tangible net assets, client relationships, trade name, deferred tax and residual goodwill.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 3: Build the pro forma income bridge

Combine standalone results, financing cost, amortisation, synergies and integration charges. Keep recurring and one-off effects separate.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 4: Build the balance-sheet bridge

Show new debt/equity, acquired assets and liabilities, intangibles, deferred tax and goodwill.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 5: Build the cash-flow bridge

Show consideration, financing, fees, integration spend, tax and operating cash; explain non-cash amortisation.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 6: Assess ratios and headroom

Calculate leverage, interest cover, liquidity and covenant headroom for base and downside cases.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 7: Create the tax diligence list

Cover filings/audits, nexus, indirect tax, payroll, transfer pricing, losses/credits, financing deductibility and post-close reporting.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 8: Separate cash tax from accounting tax

Reconcile statutory rate to effective tax rate and flag items that require local advice.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 9: Write the board challenge paper

State key unknowns, downside, sensitivity, required warranties/indemnities and approval conditions.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

## Deliverable

Pro Forma Impact Pack and Board Challenge Paper

## Acceptance criteria

The pack ties across sources/uses and statement bridges, labels training assumptions, distinguishes cash/non-cash and recurring/one-off items, and lists unresolved tax evidence.

## Reflection and debrief questions

1. Which assumption changed your recommendation most, and why?
2. What evidence would you request before relying on the result?
3. Which stakeholder owns the next decision or mitigation?
4. How does this activity feed the next stage of the M&A lifecycle?
