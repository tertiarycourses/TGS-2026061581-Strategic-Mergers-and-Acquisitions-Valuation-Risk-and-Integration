# Activity 6: Bonadio–TGA M&A Risk Intelligence Activity

**TSC mapping:** A2 · A4 · A5 · A6  
**Objective:** Use BI to assess and manage M&A risk across the lifecycle (A2, A4, A5, A6)  
**Expected output:** Risk Intelligence Dashboard and Independent Risk Committee Memo  
**Primary source:** https://www.mdpi.com/2227-9091/12/9/143

## Goal

Apply the MDPI risk themes and the SSRN enterprise-risk framework to the real announced deal, then create a decision dashboard and escalation memo.

## Real-world scenario

On 6 August 2026, CPA Practice Advisor reported that The Bonadio Group would add T. Gschwender & Associates (TGA), a Syracuse provider of credit-risk management services to financial institutions. The transaction was expected to close on 16 August 2026; financial terms were not disclosed. TGA was to join Bonadio's internal audit business unit within Advisory & Consulting.

An independent Risk Committee must challenge the deal before signing. It needs a concise view of what could prevent value creation, how exposure will be reduced and what information would trigger escalation.

## Evidence boundary

Reported deal facts and learner assumptions must never be mixed. Financial terms not disclosed by the parties are treated only as training assumptions.

### Training assumptions / guardrails

- The MDPI review identifies four connected themes: information asymmetry; performance/reputation; litigation/investor protection; geopolitical factors.
- The SSRN review discusses financial, operational, strategic, regulatory and cultural risk and structured identification/quantification methods.
- Risks used in this case are hypotheses for diligence, not allegations about either firm.

## Questions

1. How do information asymmetry and reputation risk interact in a regulated advisory business?
2. Which causes sit behind client loss, specialist-talent loss and regulatory-quality failure?
3. What controls reduce likelihood and what recovery actions reduce impact?
4. Which metrics and thresholds should trigger escalation?

## Detailed activity procedure

### Step 1: Define the risk taxonomy

Map financial, operational, strategic, regulatory/legal, cultural, information, reputation and external risks to M&A phases.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 2: Run Fishbone analysis

For three top events, identify causes across people, process, technology, data, market and governance.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 3: Write cause-event-consequence statements

Avoid vague labels. Example format: Because [cause], [event] may occur, leading to [consequence].

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 4: Score inherent exposure

Use defined 1–5 likelihood and impact scales; add velocity and detectability when useful.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 5: Design controls and deal protections

Map diligence tests, conditions precedent, warranties, indemnities, escrow, retention and integration controls.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 6: Score residual exposure

Re-score only after a named control owner and evidence of design/operation are specified.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 7: Build bow-tie views

For the top risks show preventive controls before the event and recovery controls after the event.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 8: Design the BI dashboard

Include client concentration/retention, critical-talent status, quality findings, data incidents, synergy milestones and unresolved diligence.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 9: Set thresholds and escalation

Define green/amber/red limits, decision owner, meeting cadence and required action for each indicator.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 10: Write the independent memo

Recommend go, conditional go or no-go; state residual risks, conditions and monitoring.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

## Deliverable

Risk Intelligence Dashboard and Independent Risk Committee Memo

## Acceptance criteria

Risk statements are specific, scoring scales are defined, controls have owners, dashboard thresholds are actionable and the recommendation follows the evidence.

## Reflection and debrief questions

1. Which assumption changed your recommendation most, and why?
2. What evidence would you request before relying on the result?
3. Which stakeholder owns the next decision or mitigation?
4. How does this activity feed the next stage of the M&A lifecycle?
