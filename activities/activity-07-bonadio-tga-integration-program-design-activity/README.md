# Activity 7: Bonadio–TGA Integration Program Design Activity

**TSC mapping:** A2 · A3 · A4  
**Objective:** Develop an implementation plan with stakeholders and governance (A2, A3, A4)  
**Expected output:** Integration Program Blueprint and 100-Day Roadmap  
**Primary source:** https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-gl/insights/mergers-acquisitions/documents/ey-nine-key-steps-to-setting-up-merger-acquisition-integration-program.pdf

## Goal

Use EY's nine-step integration framework to turn the Bonadio–TGA deal thesis and diligence findings into a Day One and first-100-days program.

## Real-world scenario

On 6 August 2026, CPA Practice Advisor reported that The Bonadio Group would add T. Gschwender & Associates (TGA), a Syracuse provider of credit-risk management services to financial institutions. The transaction was expected to close on 16 August 2026; financial terms were not disclosed. TGA was to join Bonadio's internal audit business unit within Advisory & Consulting.

The CEO asks you to stand up the integration program. The plan must protect regulatory quality and client service while enabling the reported capability expansion.

## Evidence boundary

Reported deal facts and learner assumptions must never be mixed. Financial terms not disclosed by the parties are treated only as training assumptions.

### Training assumptions / guardrails

- The exercise occurs before the expected close, so integration planning must respect confidentiality and gun-jumping restrictions.
- TGA is expected to join Bonadio's internal audit business unit; detailed operating-model decisions are not publicly reported.
- Learner choices are recommendations based on the deal thesis and risk analysis.

## Questions

1. Which value drivers and guiding principles should govern integration decisions?
2. What belongs with SteerCo, IMO and functional workstreams?
3. What must be ready on Day One, and what should be phased?
4. How will the team prove that value is captured without weakening quality?

## Detailed activity procedure

### Step 1: Confirm value drivers and principles

Translate the deal thesis into measurable value drivers and 4–6 decision principles, including client continuity and professional quality.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 2: Document legal interaction guardrails

Define clean-team access, confidentiality, allowed planning, prohibited pre-close control and escalation to counsel.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 3: Design three-tier governance

Name SteerCo, integration leader, IMO and workstreams; define decisions, thresholds, cadence and escalation.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 4: Create RACI and decision log

Assign one accountable owner per major outcome; record assumptions, decisions, dissent, owner and due date.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 5: Draft workstream charters

For Client/Commercial, People/Culture, Service/Quality, Technology/Data, Finance/Tax and Legal/Risk, define scope, outcomes, dependencies and resources.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 6: Define Day One must-haves

List leadership, employee/client communication, access, authority, controls, cyber, continuity and critical vendor actions.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 7: Design the target operating model

State what is absorbed, preserved or shared across people, process, technology and governance.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 8: Build the first-100-days roadmap

Sequence stabilise, validate baseline, mobilise synergies, integrate priority capabilities and embed controls.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 9: Set synergy governance

Create initiative charters with owner, finance validator, baseline, milestone, benefit, cost-to-achieve, risk and status.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 10: Create the program dashboard

Track Day One readiness, milestones, decisions, risks, dependencies, clients, talent, quality and value.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

## Deliverable

Integration Program Blueprint and 100-Day Roadmap

## Acceptance criteria

The blueprint maps all nine EY steps, separates pre-close planning from post-close control, assigns governance and owners, and links integration actions to value and risk.

## Reflection and debrief questions

1. Which assumption changed your recommendation most, and why?
2. What evidence would you request before relying on the result?
3. Which stakeholder owns the next decision or mitigation?
4. How does this activity feed the next stage of the M&A lifecycle?
