# Activity 8: Bonadio–TGA Culture, Talent and Client Continuity Activity

**TSC mapping:** K2 · A2 · A3  
**Objective:** Align stakeholders and manage culture/change in professional-services integration (K2, A2, A3)  
**Expected output:** People, Culture and Client Continuity Playbook  
**Primary source:** https://www.financierworldwide.com/briefing-room-ma-in-the-professional-services-sector

## Goal

Use the professional-services M&A briefing to design a people-and-client integration plan that preserves specialist identity while enabling scaled capabilities.

## Real-world scenario

On 6 August 2026, CPA Practice Advisor reported that The Bonadio Group would add T. Gschwender & Associates (TGA), a Syracuse provider of credit-risk management services to financial institutions. The transaction was expected to close on 16 August 2026; financial terms were not disclosed. TGA was to join Bonadio's internal audit business unit within Advisory & Consulting.

The integration team must protect TGA's specialist expertise and trusted client relationships while introducing Bonadio governance, reporting and cross-selling. A rushed absorption could undermine the capability the buyer sought.

## Evidence boundary

Reported deal facts and learner assumptions must never be mixed. Financial terms not disclosed by the parties are treated only as training assumptions.

### Training assumptions / guardrails

- The briefing emphasises that professional-services value depends heavily on people, client relationships, specialist identity and culture.
- No talent, client or culture problems have been reported for the Bonadio–TGA transaction; the risks below are planning hypotheses.
- Retention should combine financial alignment, career opportunity, clarity and cultural fit.

## Questions

1. Which aspects of TGA's specialist identity should be preserved, and which should standardise?
2. Who are the critical talent and client relationships, and what would make them leave?
3. How should leadership explain the transaction to staff and clients?
4. Which leading indicators show culture or client value is deteriorating?

## Detailed activity procedure

### Step 1: Map stakeholder impact

For leaders, specialists, wider staff, clients, regulators and partners, record needs, concerns, influence, desired behaviour and owner.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 2: Profile culture and operating norms

Compare decision style, risk appetite, client ownership, quality standards, incentives, collaboration and career paths.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 3: Choose integration posture by capability

For each area decide absorb, preserve, symbiose or hold; link the choice to the value thesis and risk.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 4: Identify critical talent

Map role criticality, client dependence, scarcity, succession, flight risk and retention action without assuming money is sufficient.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 5: Design retention and progression

Combine rollover/long-term incentives where relevant, role clarity, career path, leadership access, development and recognition.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 6: Build the client continuity plan

Name relationship owners, conflict/independence checks, service assurances, outreach timing, escalation and cross-sell guardrails.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 7: Write the change narrative

Explain why the deal, what changes, what stays, when decisions occur, how questions are answered and what cannot yet be disclosed.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 8: Create the communication cascade

Sequence leaders, employees, clients, regulators/partners and public messaging; use approved scripts and feedback channels.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 9: Build the pulse dashboard

Track regretted attrition, critical roles, engagement, client retention, complaints, service levels, cross-sell quality and conduct.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 10: Run a 30-day scenario test

Respond to a key specialist resignation plus a top-client concern; update ownership, mitigation and communication.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

## Deliverable

People, Culture and Client Continuity Playbook

## Acceptance criteria

The playbook distinguishes preservation from standardisation, names owners and indicators, covers critical talent and clients, and includes a tested response to an early warning.
