# Activity 2: Bonadio–TGA Valuation Triangulation Lab

**TSC mapping:** K3 · A1  
**Objective:** Apply business valuation concepts and reconcile a valuation range (K3, A1)  
**Expected output:** Valuation Workbook and one-page Valuation Range Memo  
**Primary source:** https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2026/08/06/credit-risk-management-firm-joins-the-bonadio-group/188098/

## Goal

Build a screening valuation for the real announced transaction using clearly labelled training assumptions because the deal's financial terms were not disclosed.

## Real-world scenario

On 6 August 2026, CPA Practice Advisor reported that The Bonadio Group would add T. Gschwender & Associates (TGA), a Syracuse provider of credit-risk management services to financial institutions. The transaction was expected to close on 16 August 2026; financial terms were not disclosed. TGA was to join Bonadio's internal audit business unit within Advisory & Consulting.

The deal sponsor asks for an indicative range before committing more resources. Your model must make uncertainty visible and must not present simulated inputs as Bonadio or TGA disclosures.

## Evidence boundary

Reported deal facts and learner assumptions must never be mixed. Financial terms not disclosed by the parties are treated only as training assumptions.

### Training assumptions / guardrails

- Training case only: FY2026 revenue US$8.0m; normalised EBITDA US$2.0m; cash tax 24%; capex US$0.20m; change in NWC US$0.10m.
- Five-year revenue growth: 8%, 7%, 6%, 5%, 4%; EBITDA margin gradually rises from 25% to 27%.
- DCF base: WACC 12%; terminal growth 3%; net debt US$0.50m.
- Market cross-check range: 7.0x–9.0x normalised EBITDA. These are course assumptions, not reported facts.

## Questions

1. What adjustments are needed to move reported EBITDA to normalised EBITDA?
2. What value range emerges from DCF and the 7.0x–9.0x multiple cross-check?
3. How does enterprise value bridge to equity value?
4. Which two assumptions drive the widest value change?

## Detailed activity procedure

### Step 1: Create an Assumptions block

Enter every training input in a dedicated blue-font input cell with units, source type and an explicit 'Training assumption' flag.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 2: Normalise EBITDA

Start from US$2.0m and add separate rows for owner/related-party, one-off and run-rate adjustments. Keep the base case at US$2.0m unless you can justify a change.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 3: Forecast operating performance

Build revenue, EBITDA, cash tax, capex and change-in-NWC rows for five years; calculate unlevered free cash flow.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 4: Calculate DCF value

Discount each year's FCF at 12%; calculate Gordon-growth terminal value at 3%; discount terminal value to present.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 5: Build the equity bridge

Start with enterprise value, subtract debt-like items, add cash if assumed and state resulting equity value.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 6: Add market cross-checks

Apply 7.0x, 8.0x and 9.0x to normalised EBITDA; explain why scale, growth, recurring revenue and key-person dependence affect positioning.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 7: Create a two-way sensitivity

Vary WACC from 10%–14% and terminal growth from 2%–4%. Each cell must recalculate the DCF.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 8: Reconcile to a range

Explain differences across methods; present low/base/high, implied EBITDA multiple and FCF yield.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 9: Perform model checks

Confirm FCF components tie, terminal value is discounted, EV-to-equity bridge works and no output is hard-coded.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

## Deliverable

Valuation Workbook and one-page Valuation Range Memo

## Acceptance criteria

The model is formula-driven, shows sources/assumptions, has a working sensitivity table and reconciles DCF with the multiple range without claiming undisclosed deal facts.

## Reflection and debrief questions

1. Which assumption changed your recommendation most, and why?
2. What evidence would you request before relying on the result?
3. Which stakeholder owns the next decision or mitigation?
4. How does this activity feed the next stage of the M&A lifecycle?
