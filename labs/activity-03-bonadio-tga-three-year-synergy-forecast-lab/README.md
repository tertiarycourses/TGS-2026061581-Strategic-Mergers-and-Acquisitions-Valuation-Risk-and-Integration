# Activity 3: Bonadio–TGA Three-Year Synergy Forecast Lab

**TSC mapping:** K2 · A1 · A2 · A5  
**Objective:** Forecast business impact and assess effects on financial performance (K2, A1, A2, A5)  
**Expected output:** Three-Year Synergy Model, Synergy Register and Management Dashboard  
**Primary source:** https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2026/08/06/credit-risk-management-firm-joins-the-bonadio-group/188098/

## Goal

Translate the public strategic rationale into a three-year base/downside/upside forecast with a traceable synergy bridge.

## Real-world scenario

On 6 August 2026, CPA Practice Advisor reported that The Bonadio Group would add T. Gschwender & Associates (TGA), a Syracuse provider of credit-risk management services to financial institutions. The transaction was expected to close on 16 August 2026; financial terms were not disclosed. TGA was to join Bonadio's internal audit business unit within Advisory & Consulting.

Leadership wants to know whether the capability combination can generate value after ramp-up, investment and talent/client risks. Finance must independently validate the sponsor's case.

## Evidence boundary

Reported deal facts and learner assumptions must never be mixed. Financial terms not disclosed by the parties are treated only as training assumptions.

### Training assumptions / guardrails

- Use Activity 2's course-only financial base; none of the financial inputs are reported transaction facts.
- Potential value levers may include cross-selling credit-risk, loan-review and regulatory-advisory services across Bonadio's financial-institution clients.
- Model base synergies: revenue US$0.25m/0.60m/1.00m; cost US$0.10m/0.25m/0.35m in Years 1–3.
- Model cost-to-achieve: US$0.80m in Year 1 and US$0.30m in Year 2; use 24% cash tax.

## Questions

1. Which synergy levers are buyer-specific and incremental to standalone performance?
2. What is the Year 3 run-rate synergy and cumulative cash realisation?
3. How do downside assumptions affect NPV, ROIC and payback?
4. Which leading indicators should the IMO monitor before financial benefits appear?

## Detailed activity procedure

### Step 1: Lock the standalone baseline

Bring in Activity 2 revenue, EBITDA and FCF. Save a versioned baseline that will not move with later underperformance.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 2: Create the synergy register

For each revenue and cost lever record formula, owner, milestone, start date, ramp, dependency, risk and cost-to-achieve.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 3: Model revenue synergies

Separate cross-sell pipeline, conversion, average revenue and delivery capacity. Do not enter one unsupported lump sum.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 4: Model cost synergies

Separate procurement, systems, facilities and shared services; prevent double counting with standalone improvement.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 5: Model dis-synergies and investment

Add client attrition, talent retention, duplicated systems and integration-team costs.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 6: Calculate after-tax cash impact

Translate benefits and costs into cash by year and calculate cumulative cash, payback, NPV and ROIC.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 7: Build downside and upside cases

Downside: 50% revenue synergy, 75% cost synergy, 25% higher cost-to-achieve and one-year delay. Upside must also be evidence-based.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 8: Design the BI dashboard

Show baseline, forecast, achieved, at-risk and lost value; include pipeline conversion, retention, milestone and spend KPIs.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 9: Run Finance challenge

Identify unsupported assumptions, timing gaps and dependencies; revise the base case and document changes.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

## Deliverable

Three-Year Synergy Model, Synergy Register and Management Dashboard

## Acceptance criteria

Standalone and synergy cases are separate; every synergy has an owner and timing; cash impact includes cost-to-achieve and tax; downside results are visible.

## Reflection and debrief questions

1. Which assumption changed your recommendation most, and why?
2. What evidence would you request before relying on the result?
3. Which stakeholder owns the next decision or mitigation?
4. How does this activity feed the next stage of the M&A lifecycle?
