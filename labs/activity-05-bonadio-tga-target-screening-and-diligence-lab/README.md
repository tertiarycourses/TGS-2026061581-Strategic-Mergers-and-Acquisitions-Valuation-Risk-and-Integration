# Activity 5: Bonadio–TGA Target Screening and Diligence Lab

**TSC mapping:** K4 · A4 · A6  
**Objective:** Use industry intelligence to screen a target and plan risk-based diligence (K4, A4, A6)  
**Expected output:** Target Screening and Diligence Workplan  
**Primary source:** https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2026/08/06/credit-risk-management-firm-joins-the-bonadio-group/188098/

## Goal

Convert the public transaction rationale into a target-screening scorecard and a diligence plan that tests specialist capability, client trust and regulatory quality.

## Real-world scenario

On 6 August 2026, CPA Practice Advisor reported that The Bonadio Group would add T. Gschwender & Associates (TGA), a Syracuse provider of credit-risk management services to financial institutions. The transaction was expected to close on 16 August 2026; financial terms were not disclosed. TGA was to join Bonadio's internal audit business unit within Advisory & Consulting.

The deal team must prepare a red-flag diligence sprint before expected close. Because the target advises regulated financial institutions, independence, data security, professional quality and client continuity are central.

## Evidence boundary

Reported deal facts and learner assumptions must never be mixed. Financial terms not disclosed by the parties are treated only as training assumptions.

### Training assumptions / guardrails

- Verified facts include TGA's reported service areas, founding year, target client type and planned Bonadio business-unit placement.
- Client concentration, financial performance, staff roster, contracts, cyber posture and regulatory findings are unknown and must be requested.
- The exercise tests whether the announced rationale is supported; it does not assert undisclosed findings.

## Questions

1. Which screening criteria best test the announced capability-expansion thesis?
2. What evidence would confirm service quality and sticky client relationships?
3. Which diligence items can change value, structure or the decision?
4. What must be handed from diligence to the integration team?

## Detailed activity procedure

### Step 1: Create the target archetype

Define required services, clients, talent, geography, quality, technology, economics and cultural characteristics before scoring TGA.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 2: Build weighted screening criteria

Assign transparent weights totalling 100%; score strategic fit, financial quality, capability, risk, culture and executability.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 3: Separate evidence levels

Tag each score as public evidence, management claim, data-room evidence, third-party evidence or unresolved.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 4: Design commercial diligence

Request client cohorts, concentration, retention, pipeline, pricing, win/loss, market and competitor evidence.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 5: Design financial and tax diligence

Request QoE, working capital, cash conversion, debt-like items, commitments, returns, audits and uncertain positions.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 6: Design regulatory and legal diligence

Review licences, independence, professional standards, complaints, litigation, contracts, privacy and regulatory interactions.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 7: Design operations, technology and cyber diligence

Review delivery workflows, quality controls, systems, data access, resilience, vendors, incidents and remediation.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 8: Design people and culture diligence

Map leaders, critical talent, incentives, succession, attrition, engagement, decision style and client ownership.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 9: Prioritise red flags

Classify each request as confirmatory, value-changing, term-changing or deal-breaking; name an owner and deadline.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

### Step 10: Create the integration handoff

Convert material findings into Day One controls, retention actions, synergy constraints and post-close monitoring.

**Checkpoint:** Record the evidence, calculation or decision produced in this step.

## Deliverable

Target Screening and Diligence Workplan

## Acceptance criteria

Weights total 100%, scores cite evidence status, every high-risk item has an owner and decision impact, and diligence findings flow into integration actions.

## Reflection and debrief questions

1. Which assumption changed your recommendation most, and why?
2. What evidence would you request before relying on the result?
3. Which stakeholder owns the next decision or mitigation?
4. How does this activity feed the next stage of the M&A lifecycle?
